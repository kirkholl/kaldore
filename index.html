About Kaldore

Kaldore is a chemicals and industrial products supplier based in Jacksonville, Florida. We connect global producers with manufacturers who need dependable supply, and we take responsibility for the part in between: sourcing, quality, storage, compliance and delivery.

The team comes from chemical distribution, polyurethane formulation and industrial logistics, so technical questions about grade, compatibility and substitution are answered by people who have handled the material.
